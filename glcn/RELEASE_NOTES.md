## What's new

### ✨ New
- 🗂️ **Several playlists at once: tap them in the picker to tick or untick; none ticked means all**
- 🔁 **A channel that will not play is swapped for the same channel elsewhere in its playlist, with a note saying which one**
- 🏀 **Liga ACB and EuroLeague with calendar, results, live quarter and table**
- 🎙️ **Voice search: say "Pon Neox en la tele" and it searches and sends it to your TV**
- 🔄 **Automatic sync between your paired devices, with a switch to turn it off**
- 📺 **Watch on TV: send a channel, film or series to your TV**
- 🎞️ **Trailers in your language first, when there is one**
- 🧭 **Guide matches channels like "ES-4 La 1 HD" by itself**
- 🏟️ **Watch live: only channels airing the match**
- 🔀 **Based on OwnTV Mobile 1.1.1: playlist edit, list position, RTL tablets, playback fixes**
