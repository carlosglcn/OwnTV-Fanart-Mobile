## What's new

### ✨ New
- 📺 **Live player: the programme is a card above the bar with its picture, time left, progress, synopsis and what follows; a Quality button switches between copies of a channel**
- 🔄 **Automatic sync between your paired devices while the app is open, with a switch**
- 📺 **Watch on TV: send a channel, film or series to your TV**
- 🎙️ **Voice search: say "Pon Neox en la tele" and it searches and sends it to your TV**
- 🗂️ **Several playlists at once: tick them in the picker; none ticked means all**
- 🔁 **A channel that will not play is swapped for the same channel elsewhere in its playlist**
- 🏀 **Liga ACB and EuroLeague, 🤾 Liga ASOBAL, 🏎️ Formula 1 and MotoGP with classification, pit stops and race-director notes, 🎾 tennis photos**
- 🎞️ **Trailers in your language first, when there is one**
- 🎯 **TMDB ID field in "Set TMDB name" for an exact match**
- 🔊 **Volume "Use for all"**
- ✨ **Lumen Night and Lumen Day styles**
- 📺 **TV Guide: the mini player is a thin bar above the tabs, so it no longer covers the grid**

### 🔧 Fixes
- 👤 **Switching profile from More no longer overlaps the page, and a new profile can be typed again**
- 🧩 **Edit playlist works again over a picture background**
- ⭐ **Favourites keep their own order per playlist**
- 🧭 **The guide matches channels like "ES-4 La 1 HD" by itself**
