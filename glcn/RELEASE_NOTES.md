## What's new

### ✨ New
- 💬 **Live TV: teletext subtitles (page 888) — Movistar+, La 1 and most Spanish channels — on the standard player**
- ⚡ **Player: a quick zap panel (list button or swipe up) with what is on, what comes next and a search across your playlists**
- 🎮 **More › TV remote: the phone as your TV's remote and keyboard; "Watch on TV" button in the player**
- 🧩 **New home-screen widget "On now in your favourites"**
- ⏰ **Home: "Starting soon" row, and a tall edge-to-edge Featured cover**
- 📁 **Favourites groups made on the TV show as folders under Favourites**
- 🎯 **Films: each file opens on the player that suits it; Dolby Vision profile 7 plays as 8.1**
- 🏷️ **Quality badges on films and series (4K, HDR, Dolby Vision, REMUX, Atmos…)**
- 🤾 **Sports: more handball leagues, cycling, snooker, tennis rankings, live race order and country presets; Watch live lists the surest channels first**
- 🗣️ **Trailers in your language first**
- 💬 **About: the OwnTV Fanart Telegram group with its QR code**
- 🔄 **From the original OwnTV Mobile (thanks, ahXN00): category and Settings PIN locks, guide priority, cleaner channel names and starting on the last channel**

### 🔧 Fixes
- 🔈 **Live TV on mpv in stereo: Dolby 5.1 channels no longer freeze**
- 📺 **Live TV: when a guide lists two programmes at once, every screen names the same one, and "Up next" follows it**
- 🔐 **Emby / Jellyfin and WebDAV: newer certificates, servers that leave out the intermediate certificate, your DNS and proxy**
- 🖼️ **Guide pictures are kept again on every update, and wide pictures are preferred over posters**
- 🆕 **Movies and Series open sorted by date added**
- ⏰ **Habit reminders come one at a time**

### 🙏 Thank you
Thank you to everyone in the community who reports bugs and shares ideas — many of the fixes and improvements in this version come from your messages.
