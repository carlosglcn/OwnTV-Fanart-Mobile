## Beta

Versión de pruebas de OwnTV Fanart. Se instala al lado de la normal.
- 🎞️ **Trailers in your language first, when there is one**
- 🔄 **Automatic sync between your paired devices while the app is open**
- 🔘 **Switch to turn automatic sync on or off**
- 📺 **Watch on TV: send a channel, film or series from your phone to your TV**
- 🧭 **Guide matches channels like “ES-4 La 1 HD” by itself**
- 🏟️ **Watch live: only channels airing the match (or about to) — no more “usually airs” rows**
- 🎙️ **Voice search: tap the mic in Search and say “Pon Neox en la tele” — it searches, and sends it to your TV when you say “en la tele”**
- 🗂️ **Several playlists at once**: tap playlists in the picker to tick or untick them; none ticked means all
- 🔁 **A channel that will not play is swapped for the same channel elsewhere in its playlist**, with a note saying which one
- 🏀 **Liga ACB and EuroLeague** with calendar, results, live quarter and table
- 🧩 **Edit playlist** works again over a picture background, and its pickers open above the form
- 🤾 **Handball**: Liga ASOBAL with calendar, results, live score and the table
- 🏎️ **Formula 1**: a session finds its channel when the guide calls it "F1"
- 🎯 TMDB info: the 'Set TMDB name' sheet has an optional TMDB ID field; with an ID the film or series is matched exactly, with no search by name and year.
- ⭐ Favourites: ordering the favourites of one playlist no longer scrambles the order of the others.
- ✨ New Lumen style (Settings → Style): Lumen Noche (near-black glass) and Lumen Día (pearl glass) with a cool blue accent.
- 🔊 Volume card: new "Use for all" button sets the volume you chose as the default for every channel and film that has no volume of its own, so you no longer have to open the card on each one.
