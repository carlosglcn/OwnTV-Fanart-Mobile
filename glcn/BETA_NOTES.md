## Beta

Versión de pruebas de OwnTV Fanart. Se instala al lado de la normal.
- 🔄 Merged the original app's latest (Nov 19–20) and its newest core: catch-up decodes in hardware and reopens by itself when it drops frames, a per-playlist "Prefer HLS for catch-up" switch, subtitles from a folder, search and A–Z in every alphabet, a notice while the database is converted after an update, per-profile sizes and recent searches, Ukrainian and Central Kurdish. Thanks, ahXN00.
- 🔄 Merged the original app's latest (Nov 21 evening) and its newest core: catch-up on Flussonic servers from the VOD playlist, the catch-up guide fills from the playlist when the feed is stale, "Prefer HLS for catch-up" on M3U playlists, guide matches re-sync the feed when needed, Downloads opens on Recordings. Thanks, ahXN00.
- 🔥 A new style, Brasa: warm graphite with an amber accent, first in the style list.
- 🌅 A light style with the gradient accent, Alba: warm ivory, orange to raspberry.
- 📺 **The guide keeps your playlist's own channel ids:** the automatic matching no longer replaces a channel's tvg-id, and only applies name matches it is sure of, with the same numbers (no more La Séptima showing La 1)
- ☕ Ko-fi: a card in About under the Telegram groups (it opens the page), and a "A coffee? Ko-fi" button in the star sheet — never a prompt of its own.
- 🎨 **Appearance, as on the TV:** Settings › Fanart starts with the Theme (Ría, Brasa, Lumen, Aurora, Crepúsculo, Estudio, Neón, Vitrina, each with a night and a day side) and the Mode (Automatic: light by day, dark at nightfall; Dark; Light). The earlier styles stay under "Other styles".
- 🔄 A switched-off playlist's own guide no longer downloads on opening the app.
- 🤾 Handball Bundesliga and the Hungarian league get their logos.
- ✅ Episodes are marked watched in the end credits (the last 7 %, never under 1:30), as on the TV.
- 📺 **Emby/Jellyfin → Trakt:** an episode or film OwnTV counts as watched (now in the end credits) is marked watched on the Emby server itself, so Emby's Trakt plugin adds it to your history — before, Emby only marked it past 90 % of a stopped report, which leaving in the credits or going on to the next episode never reached. Jellyfin too (its reports now carry the header newer Jellyfin asks for).
- 🎾 **Find a player:** Sports › My teams' search now finds tennis players and F1/MotoGP drivers and riders too (they were left out of the search), and searches the whole catalogue as well as your leagues.
- 🏁 **Dakar and WRC in Sports (Motorsport):** the Dakar Rally (cars and bikes, each as a stage race — every stage with its route and kilometres, the top three and the leader, the general classification with gaps; from the official race centre; the 2027 edition appears when its data is published, the 2026 one until then) and the World Rally Championship (every rally with its surface, special stages and kilometres, the winner once run, the drivers' standings; from Wikipedia — no live stage times). Riders and drivers can be followed.
