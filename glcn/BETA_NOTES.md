## Beta

Versión de pruebas de OwnTV Fanart. Se instala al lado de la normal.

- 🔄 Brought up to date with the latest shared core.
- 🏀 Sports: EuroCup, Primera FEB (Spain's second tier) and Lega Basket Serie A (Italy) can be followed, with calendar, results and table.
- 📺 Live: a channel whose server answers with nothing moves on after 2.5 s instead of 12 s; a re-buffer right after the audio changes format reopens the channel once; audio tracks this phone can't play are marked and never forced on.
- 🎞️ mpv: when the hardware decoder refuses a stream it switches to software decoding at once.
- 🏠 Home: films you've already watched leave Recent films and the Featured carousel; the recent channels row shows programme times.
