## Beta

Versión de pruebas de OwnTV Fanart. Se instala al lado de la normal.

- 🔄 Brought up to date with the latest shared core.
- 🏀 Sports: EuroCup, Primera FEB (Spain's second tier) and Lega Basket Serie A (Italy) can be followed, with calendar, results and table.
- 📺 Live: a channel whose server answers with nothing moves on after 2.5 s instead of 12 s; a re-buffer right after the audio changes format reopens the channel once; audio tracks this phone can't play are marked and never forced on.
- 🎞️ mpv: when the hardware decoder refuses a stream it switches to software decoding at once.
- 🏠 Home: films you've already watched leave Recent films and the Featured carousel; the recent channels row shows programme times.
- 🖼️ Logos that won't load from the playlist fall back to the guide's icon for the channel (La 2, Telecinco…) instead of the generic TV icon.
- 📺 Recent channels: the same channel listed twice by a playlist shows once; a channel with no programme picture shows its logo large on a soft wash, never an empty grey card.
- 🔝 The top bar fades as it slides away while scrolling, instead of leaving its buttons cut in half over the page.
- 🏠 Home cover: a stronger wash at the foot and the channel in a small pill, so it no longer runs into the title printed on the poster.
- 🔴 "LIVE" only for real live broadcasts (a match, a "DIRECTO" programme); everything else on now says "ON AIR".
- 🏷️ Light theme: logos sit on no dark plate — only their white parts on transparency turn grey, colours stay.
- 🏆 Sports: a live match without a score yet shows its start time instead of a lone "–", and the cards say which channel shows it; "Sport on your channels" has larger logos and full-width progress.
- 📚 Library: titles under posters take two lines, and the order button uses a short name ("Recientes").
- 👥 Film page: tap a cast member for their sheet — photo, Wikipedia line, birth and age, the summary with "Read on Wikipedia", and their other films in your playlists.
- 🗂️ Playlists sheet: each playlist says what it holds (channels, or films and series), when an Xtream line ends, and a long press switches it off or on.
- 🎬 Trakt on the phone: sign in from Settings › Metadata (the code is copied and trakt.tv/activate opens), films and episodes watched to the end go to the profile's history, and the "My Trakt watchlist" row can be shown on Home.
